Football in South Africa is talked about everywhere, in taxis, in group chats, on the touchline of a Sunday league game nobody filmed. What it hasn't always had is a place where those conversations get the production, the depth and the respect they deserve.

That's what 45one is.

## What the name means

45+1. The minute where nothing should happen and everything does. The stoppage-time moment that flips a match, a season, a career. It's the part of the game we care about most: the turn, the argument, the thing you didn't see coming.

## The podcast

Every **Thursday** we release a new episode of **451**. No script that boxes us in, no takes we don't actually believe.

- **The Premier League**, the money, the mistakes, the managers on the clock
- **The PSL**, properly covered, not as an afterthought
- **Everything around it**, transfers, culture, the business of the game

Episode one went straight at the Premier League's worst business of the window and the pressure building in the PSL. That's the standard.

## Where to find us

We publish long-form on YouTube and cut it up for Instagram and TikTok. If you only have ninety seconds, we've got something for you. If you have ninety minutes, we've got that too.

> If you love football and you're tired of takes that are engineered to go viral instead of engineered to be right, you're in the right place.

## What's next

More episodes. More guests. More writing, right here on this blog, the tactical breakdowns, the transfer deep dives and the arguments that need more than a podcast segment.
